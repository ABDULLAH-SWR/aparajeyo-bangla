========================================================================
Aparajeyo Bangla (অপরাজেয় বাংলা) — Hero Video Assets Guide
========================================================================

Place cinematic background video loops for the /home hero section here:

1. hero-bangladesh.mp4 (Primary Video Asset)
   - Codec: H.264 / AAC (or silent/muted)
   - Resolution: 720p (1280x720) or 1080p (1920x1080)
   - Frame Rate: 24fps - 30fps
   - Duration: 8 to 15 seconds seamless loop
   - Target Size: < 4 MB (heavily compressed for fast first-frame load)
   - Content: Scenic aerial view of Bangladesh (Padma/Meghna river delta,
     Sundarbans mangrove waterways, or Dhaka city skyline at dusk)

2. hero-bangladesh.webm (Optional WebM Fallback)
   - Codec: VP9 or AV1 for modern web browsers

3. hero-bangladesh-poster.jpg (Optional Still Frame)
   - High quality compressed JPEG/WebP matching the first frame of the video
   - Dimensions: 1920x1080, < 250 KB

Note:
If no video file is placed in this folder, HeroSection automatically
falls back to an animated, high-resolution Sundarbans Pine & River Delta
vector contour with Ken Burns pan and pulse effects, ensuring the hero
always renders a polished, production-grade visual experience.
